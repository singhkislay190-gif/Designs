# Mitra — neighborhood discovery design project

A responsive, interactive web concept for finding small local gatherings and meeting nearby people. The visual direction is warm, editorial, and deliberately low-pressure: soft neighborhood greens, sunny event art, rounded cards, and short human copy.

## Open the prototype

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step is needed. Google Fonts load from the web; system sans-serif fallbacks are included.

## Interactions

- Filter nearby events by category.
- Save and unsave event cards for a small confirmation.
- Navigate between the discovery and “how it works” sections.
- Layout adapts for mobile and desktop; reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Design story

**Problem:** Moving through a city can feel anonymous. Finding low-pressure ways to meet people nearby often means digging through broad social feeds or event listings.

**Audience:** People who want to feel more at home in their neighborhood, including newcomers and anyone looking for easy, small plans.

**Design response:** Keep the scope local and the invitation gentle. Put walk time, event time, and neighbor attendance directly on event cards so people can decide quickly. Use warm, tactile art and friendly language to make showing up feel approachable. Keep category filters visible and the page focused on a handful of useful options.

**Visual choices:** A muted sage palette connects the experience to place and calm; marigold and terracotta add energy to event imagery. Manrope gives the headings an open, contemporary feel, while Georgia italics add a personal editorial note. High contrast buttons and clear labels support quick scanning.

**Tools represented:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript. This project can also be recreated as a Figma design system and screen flow.
